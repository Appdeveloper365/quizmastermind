Privacy Policy

App: Quiz Mastermind — an AI trivia quiz that runs entirely in your browser.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Short version: Quiz Mastermind has no accounts, no analytics and no server of its own. Everything personal stays on your device. When you generate a question, one prompt is sent to the AI provider you chose — using your API key — under that provider's own privacy policy.

1. What we store (only on your device)

  • AI provider keys (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, NVIDIA, xAI): stored in your browser's localStorage (kept on this device only, shown masked in the app — no lock PIN needed). The plaintext key never leaves your device except to call the provider you selected.
  • Quiz data: your score, streak, history and which questions you've already seen (so they don't repeat) are kept in localStorage and IndexedDB on your device.
  • Preferences: topic, difficulty and provider choices.

Clearing your browser data, or using the in-app “Clear this key” / “Forget what I've seen” controls, removes it permanently. We cannot recover anything for you.

2. What leaves your device (only when you generate a question)

To write a question, your chosen AI provider receives a short prompt (topic + difficulty + a list of recently asked questions to avoid repeats). Depending on the provider you select:

  • Google Gemini — data handled under Google's terms: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Groq — groq.com/privacy-policy
  • OpenRouter — openrouter.ai/privacy
  • NVIDIA — nvidia.com privacy policy
  • xAI — x.ai/legal/privacy-policy
  • Puter (optional) — AI calls run under your Puter account via puter.com (“user-pays”); Puter's policy: puter.com/privacy

Requests go directly from your browser to the provider. They do not pass through any server operated by Quiz Mastermind (there isn't one).

3. What we do NOT do

  • No accounts or sign-ups on this site (Puter sign-in is an account with Puter, not with us).
  • No analytics, tracking, cookies set by us, ads, or profiling.
  • No reading of your contacts, files, camera or location.

4. Children

Content is generated to be suitable for all ages, but the app is directed at general audiences and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change with it.

6. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/appdeveloper365/quizmastermind/issues.
